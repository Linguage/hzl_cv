Doctoral thesis and related projects

Dynamic adaptability and geometry control of ballastless track on long-span bridges

  • Built a vehicle–track–bridge system dynamics model covering vehicle modelling and wheel–rail interaction, incorporating track and sub-track foundation dynamics into evaluations of running safety and ride quality.
  • Developed a local-equivalent model for long-span bridges that incorporates the global dynamics of large structures while preserving local analysis accuracy and improving computational efficiency.
  • Mapped vehicle dynamic responses to track geometry states to examine the dynamic relationship between track geometry and vehicle parameters.

About Me

My engineering background, work on websites and software, and continuing interests in reading, writing, and knowledge organisation.

Welcome to my personal CV website.

My professional background spans mechanical engineering, transportation engineering, and railway system dynamics. I have worked on modelling, numerical simulation, testing, and data processing for complex engineering systems. This work has covered coupled dynamics between vehicles and flexible structures, structural vibration control, and engineering performance assessment, and has also extended into computational methods, software tools, and digital practice.

I tend to approach complex problems through their underlying physical relationships and governing mechanisms, first defining system performance goals and then examining how changes in local structures and parameters affect overall performance. Through research and industry-commissioned projects, I have contributed to a co-simulation coupler linking several dynamics platforms, joint vehicle–track testing, and the design of unattended field-testing systems. When interpreting test results, I check model assumptions against actual conditions, compare computational assessments with experimental observations, and investigate the causes of any differences.

Reading, writing, and knowledge organisation have long been important parts of my life. To meet these needs, I build personal content websites and develop tools for bilingual reading, content search, and academic typesetting. Since late 2022, I have followed large language models and used them for programming, data analysis, and knowledge retrieval. I also apply AI agents to research workflow automation, with an interest in aligning AI with domain models, industry standards, and human engineering practice.

This website brings together my education and research, software practice, papers and patents, and selected long-running projects. If you would like to get in touch, please use one of the public links below.
