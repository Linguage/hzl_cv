Engineering Analysis | Software Development and Digital Practice

I hold a Ph.D. in engineering from Southwest Jiaotong University, with a background in study and research spanning mechanical engineering, structural engineering, and system dynamics, and I use modelling, simulation, and testing in engineering work. In recent years I have continued to develop personal content websites and software tools for literature, long-form material, and publishing. Since late 2022, I have followed large language models, with particular interest in alignment with human habits and industry standards, their combination with established theories, and their use in engineering practice. Reading, writing, and knowledge organisation remain long-term interests.
