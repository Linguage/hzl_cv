Selected doctoral research

01

Doctoral research

Dynamic adaptability and geometry control of ballastless track on long-span bridges

A system dynamics framework for evaluating vehicle safety, ride quality, and track geometry on long-span bridges, covering vehicles, wheel–rail contact, track, and bridge structures.

  • Built a vehicle–track–bridge model that incorporates sub-track foundation dynamics into vehicle-response evaluation.
  • Studied local-equivalent modelling and mapped vehicle responses to track geometry states.
02

Co-simulation

Multi-platform co-simulation for high-speed maglev systems

Research on cross-platform coupling between aerodynamic, vehicle-system, and structural responses, including the train–guideway dynamics model and coupling workflow.

  • Developed a train–guideway coupled model and contributed to a multi-platform connector.
  • Linked computational fluid dynamics, vehicle-system dynamics, and bridge structural dynamics.
03

Engineering tests

Experimental validation across high-speed, heavy-haul, and urban rail

Testing experience across high-speed rail, heavy-haul rail, urban transit, and laboratory rigs, spanning coordination, acquisition, monitoring, signal processing, and control.

  • Supported joint vehicle–track dynamic tests and field-test coordination.
  • Worked on acquisition, condition monitoring, signal processing, and test-rig control.
