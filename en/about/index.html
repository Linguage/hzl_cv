About Me

My engineering background, work on websites and software, and continuing interests in reading, writing, and knowledge organisation.

Welcome to my personal CV website.

My professional background spans mechanical engineering, transportation engineering, and railway system dynamics. I have worked on modelling, numerical simulation, testing, and data processing for complex engineering systems. This work has covered coupled dynamics between vehicles and flexible structures, structural vibration control, and engineering performance assessment, and has also extended into computational methods, software tools, and digital practice.

I tend to approach a complex problem through its underlying physical relationships and governing mechanisms, then build a model around the engineering conditions. Through research and industry-commissioned projects, I have contributed to a co-simulation coupler linking several dynamics platforms, joint vehicle–track testing, and the design of unattended field-testing systems. Computational software, test systems, and field data pose different problems. Working across them has given me experience from theoretical analysis and implementation through to experimental validation, and has kept my attention on whether an engineering judgement holds under actual conditions.

In recent years, much of my software development has grown out of websites and tools that I use myself. Linguista is the main entry point for my own material and related sites. AlpacaNotes holds personal articles and daily notes; Linguista Library supports bilingual long-form reading, collected authors, and course material; Hinbox organises material gathered for later reading; and what’s old is for technical articles and research notes. The work behind these sites includes content models, search, bilingual reading, complex book structures, editing interfaces, and automated publishing.

Since late 2022, I have followed large language models and applied them to programming, literature work, cross-document analysis, and long-form material. I have developed tools around a Zotero library for browsing, attachment parsing, summarisation, and note write-back, and built batch processes that retain thematic mapping, evidence aggregation, chunking, checkpoints, and format checks. I am interested in aligning AI with human habits and industry standards, and in combining AI with established theories for use in engineering practice.

Reading, writing, and knowledge organisation remain long-term commitments. Work on articles, books, and technical sources has gradually led me to develop tools for bilingual reading, EPUB processing, LaTeX typesetting, and multi-format publishing. Some are public and others still serve my own work; all began with needs encountered in reading, research, and writing.

This website brings together my education and research, software practice, papers and patents, and selected long-running projects. If you would like to get in touch, please use one of the public links below.
